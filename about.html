The Story Begins…

Run by women, built on tradition

Kairali began in 1990 in Tripunithura, Kerala, under VKP Industries. The aim was simple: make the coconut oil our grandmothers trusted. That means oil drawn from fresh coconut milk and slowly cooked into pure ventha velichenna (വെന്ത വെളിച്ചെണ്ണ), with nothing added.

Today Kairali is wholly women-owned and women-operated, led by founder Subi Prince. Women choose the coconuts, make the oil, bottle it and send it to you.

How It's Made

From coconut to bottle, the traditional way

Fresh coconut milk, slow care and patience. No shortcuts and no chemicals.

01

Fresh Coconuts

Mature, high-grade coconuts from Kerala.

02

Coconut Milk

The coconut is grated and pressed to draw out rich coconut milk.

03

Slowly Cooked

The coconut milk cream is cooked gently by traditional methods until the pure oil separates.

04

Pure Oil, Bottled

The oil is filtered and bottled by hand, with no additives or preservatives.
