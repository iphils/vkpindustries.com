How to use Kairali

Head Massage

Warm a few drops between your palms and massage into the scalp. Leave it on for 30 minutes or overnight, then wash.

Baby Massage

Gently massage your baby's whole body before the bath, as Kerala mothers have done for generations.

Skin Care
