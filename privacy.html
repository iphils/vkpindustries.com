Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This website (vkpindustries.com) belongs to VKP Industries, the makers of Kairali Coconut Milk Oil, based in Tripunithura, Kerala. We keep data collection to a minimum. This page explains what we collect and why.

Visitor statistics (Google Analytics)

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see how people use the site. For example, it tells us which pages are viewed, which city or state visitors are from, what kind of device they use, and whether they added a bottle to their order. Google Analytics uses cookies, and Google processes this data under its own privacy policy. We do not send your name, phone number or address to Google Analytics.

You can block these statistics with your browser's privacy settings, an ad blocker, or Google's opt-out add-on. The site works the same either way.

Placing an order

  • Your order list is saved only in your own browser, so it stays there if you refresh the page. It is not sent to us until you choose to send it.
  • Delivery check: to work out the Speed Post charge, the pincode you enter is sent to the India Post pincode service (api.postalpincode.in) and OpenStreetMap (nominatim.openstreetmap.org) to find its location.
  • Your name, phone number and address are not stored on this website. When you tap "Send order on WhatsApp", they go into a WhatsApp message to us, and you can review the message before sending it. We use these details only to deliver your order and contact you about it.
  • Payments are made directly in your own UPI app. We never see your bank or card details.

Third-party services

The site also loads fonts from Google Fonts. Links to WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram and MyStore take you to those services, and their own privacy policies apply there.
